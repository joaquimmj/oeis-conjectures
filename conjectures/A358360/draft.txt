%C A358360 The five conjectures of Oudra are true; they follow from Theorems 4 and 6 of Alekseyev, Amdeberhan, Shallit and Vukusic. Theorem 4 gives a(2n) = A000989(n) and a(2n+1) = A000989(n) + 1 + A007949(2n+1), and A254046(n+1) = 1 + A007949(2n+1); the formula with A168570 follows from A168570(2j) = 1 + A007949(j) and binomial(2m+2,m+1) = binomial(2m,m)*2*(2m+1)/(m+1). Theorem 6 gives a(3n+r) = a(n) + ((3n+r) mod 2), which is the recurrence of Sum_{k>=0} (floor(n/3^k) mod 2); and the alternating sum of A051064 equals that sum after exchanging the order of summation. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
