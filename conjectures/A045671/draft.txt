%C A045671 Kimberling's conjectures are true. In w = sigma(w), sigma: 0->11, 1->1110, the n-th 0 is the last letter of the block of the n-th 1, 00 never occurs, and 1's at distance d in {1,2} give 0's at distance 2d+2; hence the positions of 1's and 0's satisfy the mex rule with b(n) = 2a(n) + 2n. The morphism is Pisot, and the exact Dumont-Thomas computation gives -(9+sqrt(17))/32 < n*r - A045671(n) < (27+3*sqrt(17))/32 and -(9+sqrt(17))/16 < n*r - A045672(n) < (27+3*sqrt(17))/16 (optimal), inside the conjectured intervals. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
