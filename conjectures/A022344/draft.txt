%C A022344 Ramsey's conjecture is true: F3 is a Fibonacci sequence (linear in F1), and with x = F3(i-1), y = F3(i) its characteristic is |y^2 - x*y - x^2|, the norm form of Z[phi], which gives |Q(F3)| = |Q(F1)|*|Q(F2)| (an identity in the initial terms); F3 is nonzero and of constant sign from some point on, so by Morrison's theorem |F3| eventually coincides with a row p of the Wythoff array, and a(p) = a(n)*a(m). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
