%C A304451 The limits a(n)/n -> 5/2 and b(n)/n -> 5/3 are true: b(n) = n + 1 + #{k: a(k) < b(n)}, so with beta+ = limsup b(n)/n and beta- = liminf b(n)/n one gets beta+ <= f(beta-) and beta- >= f(beta+) for f(x) = 3x/(3x-2), which forces beta+ = beta- = 5/3. The inequalities fail at n = 1 (a(1) = 4, b(1) = 3) and n = 2 (a(2) = 8), and hold for n >= 3 (for all n with n+1 in place of n). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
