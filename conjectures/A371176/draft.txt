%C A371176 Kotesovec's conjecture is true, and in fact lim_{n->oo} log(a(n))/log(n) = log(2)/log((1+sqrt(5))/2). Proof: by Branicky's correspondence with Schreier sets, the number of terms in [2^(m-1), 2^m) is the number of Schreier sets with maximum m, which is A000045(m); hence there are A000045(m+2) - 1 terms < 2^m, so if a(n) has m binary digits then log(n) = m*log((1+sqrt(5))/2) + O(1) and log(a(n)) = m*log(2) + O(1). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
