%C A005121 Mohapatra's conjecture is true. By induction R(n,k) = Sum_{m<n} c(n,k,m)*R(m,m), where for fixed m the vector (c(n,k,m))_k equals M^(n-m-1) applied to (1,2,...,m), with M[k][j] = k*[j >= k]; the row vector 1*M^t has entries Stirling2(j+t,j) (same recurrence), so Sum_k c(n,k,m) = Stirling2(n,m) by telescoping, and R(n,n) satisfies Lengyel's recurrence a(n) = Sum_{m<n} Stirling2(n,m)*a(m). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
