%C A081692 Kimberling's conjecture is true: in w = sigma(w), sigma: 0->01, 1->0000, the n-th 1 is the second letter of the block of the n-th 0, so 11 never occurs, consecutive 0's are at distance d in {1,2} and the corresponding 1's at distance 2 or 6 = d(d+1); positions of 0's and 1's are complementary with the n-th 1 beyond the n-th 0, so they satisfy the defining mex rule and coincide with A081692 and A081693. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
