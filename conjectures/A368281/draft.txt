%C A368281 The conjecture is true for all n: the fixed point w of 0 -> 1^n, 1 -> 1^n 0 is the concatenation of the blocks 1^n and 1^n 0, so if the prefix of length k-1 consists of j complete blocks and r letters, its sum is nj + r with 0 <= r <= n, and the number of 0's in the prefix of length k equals the partial sum up to floor(b(k-1)/n), which is the recurrence of A368282. See A366860 for details. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
