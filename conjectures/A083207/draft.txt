%C A083207 The conjecture that any 12 consecutive numbers include a Zumkeller number is true: 3*2^a is Zumkeller for a >= 1 (take {3*2^a, 2, 4, ..., 2^(a-1)}), and q*m is Zumkeller whenever q is and gcd(q,m) = 1 (multiply both halves by the divisors of m); among 12 consecutive numbers there are two consecutive multiples of 6, one of which has 3-adic valuation 1, hence is 3*2^a*m with gcd(m,6) = 1. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
