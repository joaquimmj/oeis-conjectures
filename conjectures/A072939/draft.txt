Writing d = c(k+1) - c(k): an even d becomes -d/2; an odd negative d stays and the terms increase by |d| every two steps; an odd positive d stays and the terms decrease by d while d < c(k). For odd n with n - 1 = 2^v*u, u odd, the first v steps give d = (-1)^v*u, so c(k) -> oo iff v is odd; if v is even the pair reached is (A, A+u) with A == 1 (mod u), which leads to (1, 1+u) and then to the constant 1 (as for even n). Hence a(n) = A036554(n) + 1 and a(n)/n -> 3.
