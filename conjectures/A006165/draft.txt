%C A006165 The conjectures (i)-(vii) of Peter Bala are true. With p the leading power of 2 of n >= 2, a(n) = min(n - p/2, p), and by induction a^(k)(n) = min(n - (2^k-1)*p/2^k, 2p/2^k) for n >= 2^k, a^(k)(n) = 1 for n < 2^k. Then (i)-(v) are proved with the Walnut theorem prover, and (vi) for all k >= 2 at once, by taking u = p/2^k as a second variable (it fails for k = 1, e.g., at n = 5). For (vii), n - a^(k)(n) = max((2^k-1)*p/2^k, n - 2p/2^k), so the repeated values are (2^k-1)*2^j, each repeated 2^j + 1 times (A000051: 2, 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ...; the 35 above should be 33). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
