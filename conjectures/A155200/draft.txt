%C A155200 The conjecture is true: A(x) = exp(2x)*R(x) with R(x) = exp(Sum_{n>=2} 2^(n^2) x^n/n). By Legendre, v_2(2^j/j!) = A000120(j), and every term of [x^t] R has 2-adic valuation >= t+1 (since n^2 - v_2(n) >= n+1 for n >= 2 and v_2(k!) <= k-1); hence in a(n) = Sum_t 2^(n-t)/(n-t)! * r(t) the term t = 0 has valuation A000120(n) and all others have larger valuation. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
