%C A088617 Callan's conjecture is true: the ratio T(n,k+1)/T(n,k) = (n+k+1)(n-k)/((k+1)(k+2)) is decreasing in k and, at k = x*n, tends to (1-x^2)/x^2, which equals 1 at x = 1/sqrt(2); hence the distribution of k concentrates around n/sqrt(2), and the expected number of U's is ~ n/sqrt(2). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
