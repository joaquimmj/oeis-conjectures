%C A005427 Hadjicostas's general conjecture (Jul 22 2020) is true for every k >= 2. Writing r_n = (k-1)*T(n;k) - Sum_{s<n} T(s;k) (in [0, k-2]), one has J_k(T(n;k)) = r_n + 1 for T(n;k) >= k, where J_k(m) is the survivor: for m >= k-1 at most one wrap-around happens per step, so starting from J(m0) = j <= k-1 the survivor is j + k*s >= k+1 until the first s with j + (k-1)*s > m0, which is s = ceiling((m0 - r_n)/(k-1)) = T(n+1;k) - T(n;k), where it becomes j + (k-1)*s - m0 = r_{n+1} + 1. The base case m = k follows from J_k(v) = k*v + 1 - Q(v) for v <= k-1, Q(v) being the partial sum of T at the last occurrence of v. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
