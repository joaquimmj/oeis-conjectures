%C A070864 Layman's conjecture is true; it is equivalent to the formulas of Cloitre and Curgus: a(k^2) = 2k-1 and a(k^2-i) = 2k-2-(-1)^i for 1 <= i <= 2k-2. Proof by strong induction: for n = k^2-i with 1 <= i <= 2k-3 one has a(n-1) = 2k-2+(-1)^i, so n - a(n-1) = (k-1)^2 - i' with i' = i-1+(-1)^i, which lies in the previous block; the cases n = k^2 and n = (k-1)^2+1 are checked in the same way. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
