%C A347171 The conjecture of Doche and Mendès France is true: for every n, P_n has exactly A285869(n) real roots, all simple. For y < 0 write t = sqrt(-2y) and x = (1-y)/t = 2*cos(theta); then P_n(y) = t^n*(S(n,x) - t*S(n-1,x)) with Chebyshev S, and the zeros are the solutions of n*theta + arg(exp(i*theta) - t) = k*Pi on the two branches y < -1 and -1 < y < -3+2*sqrt(2), where this phase is respectively strictly concave and strictly increasing. There are no zeros with x > 2, and for y > 0 exactly one zero if n is even and none if n is odd. See the link. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
