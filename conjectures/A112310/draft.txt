%C A112310 Adamson's conjecture is true from the second group on: for k >= 2 the k-th group consists of the n whose lazy representation has k-1 digits, and its sum is A023610(k-2). Proof: the lazy representations with L digits are the binary words of length L with no 00 and leading digit 1; such a word is 1 followed by a word of length L-1, or 10 followed by a word of length L-2, so the total number T(L) of ones satisfies T(L) = T(L-1) + T(L-2) + A000045(L+1) with T(1) = 1, T(2) = 3, which is the recurrence of A023610. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
