\begin{abstract}
In 2017 C.~Kimberling stated, in about a hundred OEIS entries, conjectures of the form
$\ell < n r - a(n) < h$, where $a(n)$ is the position of the $n$-th occurrence of a letter in a fixed point
(or limiting word) of a binary morphism and $1/r$ is the frequency of that letter, and conjectures of the form
$a(n)/n \to m$. Extending the method of our earlier note (exact computation of the extremal discrepancy via
Dumont--Thomas numeration, certified in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt d)$), we settle all such statements whose underlying
word is a fixed point or limiting word of a primitive binary morphism, a transform of the Fibonacci word, or a
block transform of the Thue--Morse word. Most conjectures are true, often with optimal bounds; thirteen bound
conjectures and eight limit conjectures are false, with explicit counterexamples.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}
For a Pisot morphism $\sigma$ on $\{0,1\}$ prolongable on $c$ and a letter $L$, the discrepancy
$D(n) = n r - a(n)$ is an affine function of the Dumont--Thomas expansion of $a(n)-1$, with weights powers of the
conjugate eigenvalue. Its supremum and infimum are the unique bounded fixed point of a contracting max/min operator
over the prefix automaton; we guess the active edges numerically, solve exactly in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt d)$, and verify
the fixed-point equations exactly (a certificate). A limiting word is the fixed point of $\sigma^2$. When an extremal
value equals an integer bound, it is not attained since $D(n)$ is irrational for $n\ge1$. For transforms of the
Fibonacci word the positions are synchronized in Zeckendorf numeration and the inequalities, with $r\in\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)$,
are decided by the Walnut prover. Limits $a(n)/n\to 1/f_L$ follow from Perron--Frobenius (primitive substitutions),
and for Thue--Morse transforms from exact factor frequencies (Perron vector of the induced block substitution).
