%C A047863 All five observations of Ralf Stephan are true. For an odd prime p, a(n) mod p is periodic with period p-1 for n >= 1: with d = ord_p(2) and zeta a primitive d-th root of unity in GF(p), a(n) == d^(-1) Sum_r g(r, n mod d) Sum_j zeta^(-jr) (1+zeta^j)^n, where 2^(k(n-k)) = g(k mod d, n mod d). One period then gives a(n) mod 5 in {1,2}, 3 | a(2n), 7 | a(6n+5), 13 | a(12n+3); evenness is clear from the terms k = 0, n. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
