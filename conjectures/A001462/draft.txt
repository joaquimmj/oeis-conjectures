%C A001462 The conjecture a(n) >= n^(phi-1) of Jianing Song is true. Let S(m) = A001463(m), the last position of m; since phi*(phi-1) = 1 it is equivalent to S(m) <= m^phi. One proves m^phi/phi <= S(m) <= m^phi by strong induction: if a(j) = v then S(v-1) < j <= S(v), which gives j^(phi-1) <= v < 1 + (phi*j)^(phi-1); summing over j <= m gives the two bounds for m > 30, and m <= 30 is checked directly. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
