%C A005590 Stephan's conjecture is true: a(3n) = 0 if and only if n is in A003714, and otherwise a(3n) is even with |a(3n)| >= 2. Proof: the vector u(n) = (a(3n), a(3n+1), a(3n+2), a(3n+3)) satisfies u(2n) = (a0, a1-a0, a1, a2-a1) and u(2n+1) = (a2-a1, a2, a3-a2, a3) in terms of u(n) = (a0, a1, a2, a3). While the binary digits of n contain no 11, u(n) is (0, 1, 1, -2m) or (0, 1, -2m-1, -2m); after the first 11 it lies in the set {a0(a0-a1) > 0, a1(a1-a2) > 0, a2(a2-a3) > 0}, which is mapped into itself by both maps and excludes a0 = 0. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
