It is a first-order statement in (k, n) over the Thue-Morse sequence, and the Walnut theorem prover confirms it for all odd k at once, both for the positions with A010060(n) + A010060(n+k) = 1 and for those with A010060(n) = A010060(n+k); for the latter see also Allouche (2015). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
