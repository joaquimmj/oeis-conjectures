%C A361190 Kotesovec's conjectured exponent follows from the theorem of Denisov and Wachtel on random walks in cones: in the coordinates (x-y, y) the steps become (0,1), (2,-1), (-1,0) in the quarter plane; the exponential change of measure with weights 1/4, 1/4, 1/2 removes the drift (growth rate 2*sqrt(2) per step, i.e., 64^n), and the resulting walk has correlation coefficient -1/sqrt(3), so the cone angle is arccos(1/sqrt(3)) = arctan(sqrt(2)) and the excursion exponent is 1 + Pi/arctan(sqrt(2)). Since this is irrational, the g.f. is not D-finite. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
