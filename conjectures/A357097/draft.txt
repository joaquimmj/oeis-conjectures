%C A357097 The conjectured formula A(A019586(x), A019586(y)) = A019586(A101330(x,y)) is true. Proof: with w = phi - 1, the product of pairs is the multiplication of j + k*w in Z[phi], and S_n = {b_n*phi^m}. For n >= 1 with Zeckendorf representation Sum F(c_i), the pair (successor of n in its Wythoff row, n) corresponds to t(n) = Sum phi^(c_i) = Sum F(c_i - 1) + n*phi. The conjugate of t(n) lies in the interval (-1/phi^2, 1/phi) of length 1, which determines t(n) among the elements a + n*phi, and the product of two numbers of this interval is again in it; since the coefficient of phi in t(x)*t(y) is the Knuth product A101330(x,y), t(x)*t(y) = t(A101330(x,y)). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
