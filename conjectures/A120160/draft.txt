%C A120160 All three conjectures of Hadjicostas are true. The Josephus one is the case k = 5 of the general theorem (see A005427). For Conjectures 1 and 2: in base 5/4 the parent of N >= 1 is 4*floor(N/5), so numbers with n digits form an interval I_n, and I_{n+1} is the union of the blocks [5M, 5M+4] over the multiples 4M in I_n; with U_n = ceiling(min I_n/4) one gets U_{n+1} = U_n + ceiling(U_n/4) and #(multiples of 4 in I_n) = U_{n+1} - U_n, i.e. U_n = Sum_{i<=n} a(i). Conjecture 1 holds when 0 is not counted (it fails only at n = 2 otherwise). - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
