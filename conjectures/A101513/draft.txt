%C A101513 Sloane's conjectures (1)-(4) are true. By induction, row n consists of the primes p >= 5 with 2p in row n-1, followed by the next composites in increasing order: a prime p is eligible exactly when 2p lies in the previous row, and every composite c is eligible because the composites of the previous row fill an interval of length > sqrt(c), which therefore contains a multiple of the least prime factor of c (explicit Montgomery-Vaughan and Rosser-Schoenfeld bounds for n >= 1500, direct computation below). Conjecture (5) is not addressed. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
