%C A118006 The functional equation of Hebisch and Rubey, as corrected by Somos, is true when f(x) = Sum_{n>=1} a(n)*x^(n-1) (it fails with x^n). Proof: the pairs of letters of the sequence (01 and 10) form the fixed point of a -> aab, b -> abb, and the identity a(n) + a(n-1) + a(n-2) - [n = 3m+3] a(m+1) = (2 if n == 4, 5 (mod 6), else 1) for n >= 2 is then proved with Walnut in base 3. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 18 2026
