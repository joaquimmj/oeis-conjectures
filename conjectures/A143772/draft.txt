%C A143772 The conjecture is true. Odd values: if m is odd every k + m/k is even; if m is even, an odd prime p dividing a(n) divides both m+1 and 2 + m/2, i.e., 3/2 == 0 (mod p), so p = 3, and similarly 9 cannot divide a(n); hence the odd values are 1 and 3. Even values: for even e take a prime q == 1 (mod e); then for primes r, gcd over the divisors of q*r is gcd(q+r, q^2-1), and choosing (Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet) a prime r == e*t0 - q (mod q^2-1) with gcd(t0, (q^2-1)/e) = 1 gives exactly e. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov 19 2026
