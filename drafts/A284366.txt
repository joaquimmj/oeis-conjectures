The conjecture is false: the upper bound 2 fails first at n = 2977771, where a(n) = 5334043 and n*s - a(n) = 2.00487... (exactly: 21*n^2 > (2*a(n)+4+n)^2). The lower bound -1 holds. The exact extreme values are inf(n*s - a(n)) = (-9+sqrt(21))/5 = -0.8834... and sup(n*s - a(n)) = (6+sqrt(21))/5 = 2.1165..., neither being attained.
