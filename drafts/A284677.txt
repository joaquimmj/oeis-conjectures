A284677, section FORMULA :

The conjecture a(n) = A284674(n-1) fails at n = 6: a(6) = 0 and A284674(5) = 1. The correct relation is a(n) = A284674(n) for n >= 3. Proof: let p: 0->1, 1->0111; then p maps each limiting word to the other. Writing a = 10t and A284674 = 01u gives u = 111p(t) and t = 111p(u), and the map w -> 111p(111p(w)) has a unique infinite fixed point, so t = u. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov xx 2026
