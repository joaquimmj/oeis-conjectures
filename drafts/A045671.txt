A045671, section COMMENTS :

Kimberling's conjecture -1 < n*r - a(n) < 2 is true. More precisely, for n >= 1, (-9-sqrt(17))/32 <= n*r - a(n) <= (27+3*sqrt(17))/32 with r = (1+sqrt(17))/4, and both bounds are best possible (they are the infimum and the supremum). Proof: Dumont-Thomas numeration for the morphism 0->11, 1->1110 expresses n*r - a(n) as a sum of g_i*c^i over the digits, where c = (3-sqrt(17))/2 is the conjugate eigenvalue; the extreme values solve a finite max/min system that is a contraction. - _Joaquim Manji-Jung_, Nov xx 2026
